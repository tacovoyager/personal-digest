Cover professional and general disc golf: the PDGA (Professional Disc Golf
Association) and the Disc Golf Pro Tour (DGPT).

- Tournaments: results from the past week's DGPT and PDGA Major events
  (MPO and FPO winners, notable finishes and storylines), and what's coming
  up next. In the off-season, say briefly where the tour stands instead.
- Players: sponsorship and team changes, injuries, suspensions or
  withdrawals, and retirements.
- Organizations: PDGA and DGPT announcements, rule and policy changes,
  schedule and broadcast news, and disputes or controversies.
- Trending topics in the wider sport: notable disc and gear releases,
  manufacturer news, new or notable courses, viral moments, and topics the
  disc golf community is widely discussing.

Sources: use pdga.com and dgpt.com for official facts (results, rules,
schedules). Prefer dedicated disc golf outlets for coverage, e.g. Ultiworld
Disc Golf, and established sports outlets when they cover the sport.
Community discussion (r/discgolf, player and media social posts, podcasts
and YouTube coverage) is valued as a signal of what's trending, but verify
facts elsewhere before stating them.
