Primary focus on German automotive, with extra attention to anything within
the Volkswagen Group, for brands such as Volkswagen, Audi, and Porsche.
Electric or ICE vehicles, no preference, but Audi S and RS models should
receive the highest attention.

For a slow news week, other German brands (BMW, Mercedes) can be used as a
fallback, and general European brands after that. Mix in some American,
Japanese, and Korean brands only if nothing else can be found.
