<!--
Research and analysis guidelines applied to every topic. Each topic's own
instructions (topics/*.md) are layered on top of these. Edit freely.
-->

Coverage window:
- Focus on news from the past week. Older stories are worth including only
  when there's a new development this week; say what changed.

Sources and accuracy:
- Prefer primary sources (official announcements, team and project sites,
  release notes) and established outlets. Treat aggregators, press releases,
  and social posts as leads to verify, not as sources, unless the topic's
  instructions say a community source is valued.
- Separate what happened from speculation. Label rumors and unconfirmed
  reports as such.
- When sources disagree, say so and summarize each side briefly.

Sports topics (topics whose instructions say they're a sports topic):
- Cover the most recent game(s): result and key takeaways. Skip this when the
  team is in its off-season, and say briefly where the team stands instead.
- Cover injuries and injury updates, trades and roster moves, and coaching or
  front-office changes.

Writing:
- Lead with what matters most. Skip minor updates and marketing.
- Be concise: a few sentences per story, plain language, no hype.
